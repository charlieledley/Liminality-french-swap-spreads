# Deck plan — French swap spreads investor deck (opened 2026-10-04)

Status: draft plan from Claude after reading Charlie's deck of 2026-10-04. Not yet agreed.

## What the draft is

36 slides, 10 x 5.625in (16:9 small). Slides 1-12 are the French opportunity; slides 13-36 are
the July 2025 US Treasury belly swap-spread deck, kept as appendix and as the template the
French pages were cloned from. Slides 3 and 6 are the same scenario slide twice. Slide 8 is a
placeholder for an Italy 2012 chart, slide 11 for the election calendar. Every chart is a pasted
picture, so the data is not in the file. The IRR figures are typed labels with no source table.

## Target format

The put-writing deck's house format: 13.333 x 7.5in, Midnight Executive palette, Cambria heads
and Calibri body, built by a python-pptx script that reads JSON written by `analysis/` scripts,
`internal_note()` for anything addressed to Charlie, `INTERNAL_NOTES = False` for the external
build, geometry QA with `deck/qa.py`, render with LibreOffice and look at every page. Native
charts, not pictures, wherever the data is ours.

Reusable from the put-writing deck as-is: title / important information / Liminality Capital
pages (firm boilerplate), the design system (`deck/dsl.py`), logos, contact page.

## Proposed running order (from the draft's own structure)

1. Title
2. Important information
3. Liminality Capital
4. Executive summary: the French opportunity (draft slide 2)
5. What is a swap spread, in euro terms (adapted from draft slide 19; OAT vs Euribor, sign stated)
6. The dislocation: 5.4y vs 1y OAT/Euribor spread since 2012 (draft slide 5, rebuilt as native chart)
7. Why is there a dislocation (draft slide 4: fiscal, political, levered unwinds, global rates)
8. What is the trade: bond + pay fixed + 4y TRS at €STR+17 with floor (draft slides 23-24, euro terms)
9. Base case: the roll from 5.4y to 1y over 4.4 years (draft slide 5)
10. Scenario analysis: 4y IRR **and MOIC** by at-expiry spread, break-even and impairment marked
    on the 1y history (draft slide 3; IRR/MOIC from Charlie's spreadsheet, history from `analysis/`)
11. Stress analogue: Italy 2011-12 (draft slide 8, to be built)
12. Risks: mark-to-market vs permanent impairment, far-left win, EU exit (draft slide 7)
13. Appendix: election odds (Polymarket picture), election calendar, disclaimer

The US belly deck (draft slides 13-36) is not carried as an appendix (Charlie, 2026-10-04).
Slides repurposed from it: 19 (what is a swap spread), 22 (why the dislocation), 23-24 (what is
the trade, TRS diagram), 26 (the roll), 27 (scenario layout), 30 (risks structure).

Swap curve for every historical chart: €STR OIS, EONIA spliced in before 2019-10-02, footnoted;
the trade's own 6m Euribor swap layered in as a later step and footnoted (decision 0003).
