1
French Political and Fiscal Risk Calendar
France enters the final months of 2026 with unusually elevated fiscal and political risk. The 
government has reduced its 2026 growth forecast to 0.5% and acknowledged that its previous 5.0%
deficit target will be missed, while debt stood at 117.5% of GDP at the end of the first quarter. The 
government is currently preparing a 2027 budget reportedly involving around €54 billion of 
savings and targeting a deficit of approximately 5% of GDP.

Late November–

2026
Senate 
consideration and 
parliamentary 
budget endgame
Assuming the Assembly completes its first reading, 
the budget proceeds to the Senate and then through 
the parliamentary navette, that is, the back and 
forth between the National Assembly and the 
Senate, making the revisions it deems necessary.
This period will determine whether France enters 
2027 with an enacted budget or again requires 
temporary arrangements. France began 2026 under 
a special finance law after Parliament failed to 
adopt the budget before year-end.
